Gamemodes

Pick the mode you play most

Each gamemode is tested and ranked on its own. A tier in one mode says nothing about the others.

    Process

    From Discord to your tier

    1. Join the Discord

      Go to discord.gg/primetiers and open the request-test channel for your gamemode.

    2. Join the queue

      Enter the waitlist for your region. Your tester will call you when they are free.

    3. Fight your tester

      Play the test on Primetiers.net. Your tester judges your whole performance, not just the score.

    4. Get placed

      Your tester posts your tier. It appears on the leaderboard for that gamemode.

    Tier scale

    HT1 is the top. LT5 is the bottom.

    Each level has a high and a low tier. Every tier has its own colour.
